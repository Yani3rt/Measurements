# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is one local household/family user managing clothing measurements for family profiles. They use the app in a personal, low-risk local context to record, review, and update measurements for clothing, tailoring, and fit reference.

## Product Purpose

The Atelier helps a household keep accurate body measurements per profile, including profile height and individual measurement values. Success means the user can quickly find the right profile, understand which measurements are saved, update values confidently, and later review measurement history without needing a hosted service.

## Brand Personality

Refined, editorial, calm, precise. The product should feel like a digital atelier or fashion-measurement notebook: crafted and personal, but still clear enough to support practical measurement work.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, noisy admin panels, clinical medical-record styling, playful toy-like family apps, and visually trendy AI-generated patterns that distract from accurate measurement work.

## Design Principles

1. Measurement clarity comes first: visual elegance should never make values, labels, or actions harder to understand.
2. Feel crafted, not generic: preserve the editorial atelier tone while keeping product interactions familiar.
3. Reduce anxiety around edits: profile and measurement changes should feel reversible, understandable, and clearly confirmed.
4. Reveal detail when useful: history, guidance, and technical measurement information should support the active task rather than compete with it.
5. Respect the local household context: optimize for a single trusted user and a personal archive, not enterprise workflows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific accessibility target was requested. Use basic readable contrast, reduced-motion support where already present, and common browser interaction patterns, but keyboard-first workflows are not a product requirement for this local single-user app.
